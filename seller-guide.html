GETTING STARTED

Five steps to your first sale

01

Submit your application

Fill out the Become a Seller form with your business details, category, and product range. Takes about five minutes.

02

Verification

Our team reviews your business documents and product authenticity. Most applications are verified within 2–3 business days.

  • CNIC or business registration
  • Bank account for payouts
  • Proof of product sourcing (invoices or brand authorization)
03

Set up your storefront

Upload your logo, write a brand description, and configure shipping zones from your seller dashboard.

04

List your products

Add product photos, sizes, pricing, and stock levels. Use our pricing benchmark tool to stay competitive in your category.

05

Start selling & get paid

Once live, your products appear in search and category pages. Orders settle to your account on a weekly cycle.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

What you'll need

Business identity

A registered business name or sole-proprietor CNIC, plus a valid contact phone and email.

Product authenticity

Proof of sourcing for branded gear — either brand authorization letters or supplier invoices.

Fulfillment capability

Ability to pack and hand off orders within 24–48 hours of purchase confirmation.

Bank account

A Pakistani bank account in the business or owner's name for weekly payout settlements.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How long does verification take?

Most applications are reviewed within 2–3 business days. You'll get an email once your account is approved.

Can I sell in more than one category?

Yes. Choose a primary category on your application, then add additional categories from your seller dashboard afterward.

Who handles shipping?

You pack and hand off orders to your chosen courier partner; KHELZONE provides pre-negotiated courier rates in the dashboard.

What if a buyer requests a return?

Returns are logged in your dashboard with the reason and photos. Approved returns are deducted from your next payout cycle.

Ready to get started?

Applications typically take five minutes to complete.
